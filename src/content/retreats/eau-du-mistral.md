Eau du Mistral is for those who feel weighed down by accumulation—too many
commitments, too much noise, too little space to breathe. Taking its spirit from
the famous wind of Provence, the retreat is designed to clear and renew.

Through reflection, creative practice, and time in a landscape made for
restoration, you'll release what has grown heavy and reconnect with a lighter,
clearer way of being. You'll leave, like the Provençal sky after the Mistral,
feeling washed clean and newly bright.
