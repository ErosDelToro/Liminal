Of all the Liminal experiences, Ascent is the most physical. In Zermatt, beneath
the Matterhorn, the days are built around real exertion: an alpine trail race,
and preparation for a high peak.

It is self-directed by design. You set your objective, arrange your own mountain
guides, and own your preparation and risk. Liminal provides the frame, the
information, and the introductions; the climbing, the guiding, and the logistics
are yours to arrange. A shared chalet and shared meals hold the group together.

The reason for the effort is embodiment. Insight that stays stuck in your head
often arrives in the body instead, at altitude and past the point of comfort.
What you take home is a clearer, first-hand sense of how you meet difficulty when
it is genuinely hard.
