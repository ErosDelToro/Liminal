Transition is rarely a single decision. It is a passage. We begin by getting
honest about where you are: what is ending, what is calling, and what is holding
you in the doorway. From there we build a way forward that respects both.

Drawing on engagement theory and the idea of ikigai, our sessions give you tools
to navigate this change and the ones that follow. The aim is a life that feels
authored rather than inherited, defined the way you choose to define it.
