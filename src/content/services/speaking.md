Great talks do two things at once: they move a room and they leave something
useful behind. Mehdi weaves personal story into research-grounded content,
reading the energy of the audience and responding to it in real time.

Whether it's a conference keynote, a corporate gathering, or a workshop, the aim
is the same—provoke thought, inspire change, and give people a genuine reason to
grow. Tell us about your event and we'll design a talk that fits it.
